FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance for weddings and major events, and 4–8 weeks for commercial projects. Peak seasons fill up quickly.
What's included in the final deliverables? +
All packages include color-graded footage, professionally edited final cut, licensed music, digital delivery via private link, and a highlight reel. Specifics vary by package.
Do you travel for projects? +
Absolutely. We've filmed across continents. Travel fees apply for destinations outside our home region — we'll include a detailed quote during consultation.
How long before I receive my final video? +
Highlight reels are typically delivered within 72 hours. Full wedding films take 4–8 weeks. Commercial projects are discussed during pre-production planning.
Can I request revisions? +
Yes. All packages include at least 2 rounds of revisions. We want you to be 100% thrilled with your final film.

Wedding Films

Your wedding day is a once-in-a-lifetime event. Our wedding videographers blend into your celebration invisibly, capturing authentic moments, emotions, and the poetry of your day in a cinematic narrative film you'll treasure forever. Full ceremony, reception, and highlight reel included.

Commercial & Brand Videos

In a crowded marketplace, your brand needs video that stops the scroll. We produce high-concept commercials, product launches, social media content, and corporate identity films that convert viewers into loyal customers. Strategy, scripting, production, and distribution guidance included.

Event Coverage

Corporate conferences, award galas, product launches, concerts, and cultural events — we deploy multi-camera rigs and seasoned operators to capture every keynote, performance, and standing ovation with broadcast-quality precision.

Music Videos

We collaborate with artists to create visually arresting music videos that amplify your sound and build your visual identity. From concept development and location scouting to choreography direction and final color grade — we handle it all.

Real Estate & Architecture

Properties sell faster and at higher prices with stunning video. We provide licensed drone operators, Matterport 3D walkthroughs, and cinematic property films that make listings irresistible to buyers in any market.

Post-Production Suite

Already have footage? Our post-production team offers color grading, sound design, motion graphics, visual effects, and full editing in our dedicated suite. We work with all formats and deliver broadcast-ready masters.

Our Story

ABOUT US

Passion-driven filmmakers. Detail-obsessed storytellers.

Founded 2016

Born from a Love
of Storytelling

Clip Crown was founded on a simple belief: every story deserves to be told beautifully. What began as a two-person team with a passion for filmmaking has grown into an award-winning production studio trusted by couples, brands, and artists around the world.

We combine technical mastery — cinema-grade cameras, precision lighting, drone cinematography — with a deep commitment to emotional storytelling. The result is work that doesn't just document moments but transforms them into art.

Our team of directors, cinematographers, and editors bring together decades of combined experience from feature films, broadcast television, and advertising. When you hire Clip Crown, you get a world-class production team fully dedicated to your vision.
